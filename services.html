How We Work

Embedded. Hands-on. Accountable.

We don't hand you a slide deck and disappear. Sandstone functions as a working partner inside your business — structured as a defined engagement with clear milestones and outcomes.

01
Diagnose
We start with a structured discovery process to understand where the business is, where it needs to go, and what's in the way.
02
Scope & Engage
We propose a defined engagement — specific deliverables, a clear timeline, and a dedicated team — structured as a flat consulting retainer.
03
Deliver
We execute alongside your team, embedded inside the business, until the outcome is achieved and the infrastructure is in place.
