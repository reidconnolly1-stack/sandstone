Common Questions

Before you reach out.

Do you take equity in exchange for your work?+
No. Sandstone is entirely fee-based. We never take equity, board seats, or any ownership stake. You retain full control of your cap table throughout and after the engagement.
What size company do you typically work with?+
Most clients fall in the $0–20M revenue range — past the earliest startup stage, but before they have the budget or need for full-time C-suite hires across finance, legal, and operations.
Can you focus on more than one outcome at once?+
Yes — many engagements blend two outcomes (for example, cutting cost while preparing for a sale). The Scale tier is built specifically for multi-outcome programs.
What happens when the engagement ends?+
We build every deliverable — dashboards, financial models, legal documents, AI tools — to run independently after we leave. Your team gets full training and documentation as part of every engagement.
How is this different from hiring a fractional CFO?+
A fractional CFO covers one function. Sandstone combines financial, legal, operational, and technical expertise inside a single coordinated project — with a dedicated Project Lead managing all of it for you.
