Where discipline

Sandstone Growth Advisors was built around a single observation: the founders who build something are not always equipped to run it at the speed the market demands. We close that gap.

Our Story

Built by operators,
not theorists.

We are five co-founders from UCLA Anderson — spanning institutional finance, capital markets, employment law, systems engineering, and operations — united by a shared conviction that integrated advisory is more valuable than fragmented vendor relationships.

Every Sandstone engagement includes a dedicated Project Lead who embeds directly in your operation. We do not advise from a distance. We build alongside you, and we leave behind tools, systems, and documentation that run on their own when the project ends.

Our first engagement — with Dymes Agency, an LA-based creator and influencer agency — became the proof point for how we work: full operational diagnostics, a custom-built client dashboard, and a financial infrastructure rebuild, delivered in weeks, not quarters.

"We don't take equity. We don't sign multi-year retainers. We get hired to solve a specific problem, and we leave once it's solved."

Outcome-Driven
Every engagement starts with a defined business outcome — scale, revenue, cost, or exit — not a vague scope of work.
Embedded, Not Advisory
We sit inside your operation as a working partner. Our Project Leads build alongside your team, not from a deck.
Fee-Based, Equity-Free
We're compensated for outcomes delivered — never equity. Your cap table stays exactly as clean as it was before we arrived.
